## 📝 Problem Statement

Given an array of integers heights representing the histogram's bar height where the width of each bar is 1&nbsp;return the area of the largest rectangle in the histogram.

### Example 1:

Input: heights = [2, 1, 5, 6, 2, 3]

Output: 10

Explanation: The largest rectangle is highlighted, which has an area of 5*2 = 10 units.

<img src="https://static.takeuforward.org/content/1791829354_gHNn3_qo.webp">

### Example 2:

Input: heights = [3, 5, 1, 7, 5, 9]

Output: 15

Explanation: The largest rectangle has an area of 5*3 = 15 units.
